Roll or assign?

For a short call or short put under pressure: is rolling worth it, or do you take the outcome (let shares get called away, or take assignment)? Encodes the common maxims: prefer credit rolls, cap debits, don't pay to dodge an outcome you're fine with.

Verdict encodes the common maxims: prefer credit rolls, keep any debit under ~1% of stock, cap rolls at ~2 before taking the outcome, and don't pay to dodge an outcome you're already fine with. It does not check liquidity, earnings, the new strike vs your share cost basis, or upcoming dividends; those stay your gates before you act. Not trading advice; you pull the trigger.
